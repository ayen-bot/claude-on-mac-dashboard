Connect once, about a minute each:

  • Click the + in Claude's message bar, hover Connectors, then Manage connectors (or Customize > Connectors).
  • Browse claude.ai/directory, click a tool, click Connect, sign in, approve permissions.
  • On Team/Enterprise, an account Owner enables it org-wide first.

Note: Claude reads and drafts email but does not send for you. It only sees what you can see.

Once connected, prompts work on your live data instead of copy-paste. Anywhere a prompt says [PASTE OR CONNECT], you can also paste by hand.
